\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages the Vulkan physical device selection, logical device instantiation ({\ttfamily Vk\+Device}), and graphics queue retrieval. 

Implements RAII semantics for automated lifecycle management of the logical device. Move operations are supported, but copy operations are disabled to prevent duplicate destruction.
